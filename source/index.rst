   <section class="teaching-home-band">
     <div class="teaching-home-band__header">
       <div>
         <p class="teaching-kicker">Teaching Approach</p>
         <h2>Learning Earth science by using models, data, and clear reasoning.</h2>
       </div>
       <p>
         My courses are built around the idea that science education should do more than deliver answers. Students should leave better able to interpret evidence, test explanations, and solve unfamiliar problems.
       </p>
     </div>
     <div class="teaching-home-practice">
       <article>
         <p class="resource-card__eyebrow">Reasoning</p>
         <h3>Evidence over memorization</h3>
         <p>Courses emphasize logic, explanation, and problem solving rather than treating science as a set of fixed answers to recall.</p>
       </article>
       <article>
         <p class="resource-card__eyebrow">Practice</p>
         <h3>Authentic scientific problems</h3>
         <p>Students work with real Earth-system data, imperfect observations, and quantitative models that feel closer to how science is actually done.</p>
       </article>
       <article>
         <p class="resource-card__eyebrow">Support</p>
         <h3>Accessible, flexible, and reflective teaching</h3>
         <p>Course materials stay organized and up to date online, help is available outside scheduled hours, and courses keep evolving in response to feedback and changing student needs.</p>
       </article>
     </div>
   </section>
